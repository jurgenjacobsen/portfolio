# SoundFX
Using [cuelume](https://cuelume.dev/), sound effects were added to increase the tactile feel. 
Here in this document I have listed a small guide of how the sounds are used in the UI.

## Cues Used
| Cues     | Condition                                                                  | Associated Components |
|----------|----------------------------------------------------------------------------|----------------------:|
| tap      | General tactile feel to buttons and other interactions                     |  `buttons, tags, etc` |
| navigate | When the user navigates across pages on the website, or on external links. |     `button, link, a` |
| success  | On user "Good" interactions. (Such as clicking on the "Contact me" button) |     `button, link, a` |
| type     | Typing sound during user interaction                                       |     `textarea, input` |
